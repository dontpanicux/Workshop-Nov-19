Set your quiet window: 1.5 to 2 times your usual gap

Your kind of businessUsual gapGone quiet after
Coffee, food, or other refills4 weeks6 to 8 weeks
Massage, physio, or other wellness visits4 weeks6 to 8 weeks
Salon or barber6 weeks9 to 12 weeks
Fitness classes1 week2 to 3 weeks
Skincare or supplements8 weeks12 to 16 weeks
Detailing or seasonal service12 weeks18 to 24 weeks

Rules of thumb. Your own orders beat this table: find a few regulars, count the weeks between their visits, and double it.

bolt.new

"Too short and you're emailing people who were about to come back anyway. Too long and they've already found someone else. Start at one and a half to two times your usual gap."

If asked who belongs on the list: regulars only. Appointments: skip anyone with a future booking. Classes: 2 to 3 missed weeks. Seasonal: compare to the same season last year. Leave off anyone who opted out or was emailed in the last 14 days.

Go live: send from your own domain

  1. Buy a domain in your project: gear icon > All project settings > Domains & Hosting > Buy a new domain. It attaches to the project right away.
  2. In Resend, open Domains, then Add Domain, and enter that domain. Not a bolt.host address.
  3. Add each record Resend shows, exactly as shown: profile icon > Settings > Cloud > Custom domains > ••• > Open domain settings > Add new record. In Name, enter only the part before your domain, like send.
  4. Wait for Verified, usually 10 to 20 minutes, sometimes a few hours. Then run Prompt 4.

Optional: add a TXT record named _dmarc with the value v=DMARC1; p=none; to help a new domain reach the inbox.

Why it matters

A verified domain proves the email is really from you, so it reaches inboxes instead of spam, and test mode can turn off.
